Magic Moment 01
New stock arrived.
Or needs to be raised. POS handles both.
When any new stock arrives at the store via Stock Transfer, the store manager opens POS on their phone. They tap through incoming inventory — batch by batch, good or damaged — and hit confirm. The warehouse system updates automatically. No calls. No email. No waiting.
  • 📦  Raise stock requests from the store — auto-converts to a warehouse transfer order
  • ✅  Receive incoming stock with good/bad capture per batch — WMS updates instantly
  • 🔄  Mark damaged inventory — moves to the correct bin in the warehouse system automatically

Magic Moment 02
A farmer walks in.
Billed and out in under 3 minutes.
The manager searches the farmer by mobile. Profile appears instantly. The farmer browses live prices, active offers, real stock. They pick what they need. The system selects the right batch automatically. Invoice gets generated and is sent on the WhatsApp number of the farmer. Payment collected — cash or digital payment via UPI. Ledger gets settled if paid digitally, directly.
  • 🔍  Search any farmer by mobile — or register a new one instantly
  • 🛒  Full catalog with live prices, offers, stock count — same data as the CRM
  • 🧾  Invoice generated will be pushed to financial system NAV-BC automatically — no manual entry
  • 💳  Cash collected will be deposited to the store VAN account — this will reconcile to the ledger automatically
  • ↩️  Returns on the same screen — condition captured, credit posted automatically
Magic Moment 03
An online DVS order just came in.
The store can handle it.
A farmer in the store's catchment placed an order on the AgroStar app. It routes directly to the nearest COCO store. The manager sees it in POS, marks it available and triggers the invoice in one tap. Returns handled on the same screen — logged, inventory updated, credit posted.
  • 📱  DVS orders routed to the store appear directly in POS — accept, flag OOS, or process
  • 🚚  Invoice is generated via POS and the order will be picked up by the LMD partner
Under the Hood
POS is not a billing app. It is the connective layer between three systems that now work as one. When a sale happens on POS, all three know about it instantly and automatically.
