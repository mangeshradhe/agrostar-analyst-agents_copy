3 · Clearance vs fresh stock

What has to move now vs what can wait

Read every number with these caveats.
  • Budget (ROFO) is a rolling plan — it only has real numbers for May–Sep right now. Apr and Oct onward are not filled in yet.
  • Sales and Procurement stop at 15 Jul 2026. July is 13 of 31 days — don't read it as a full month.
  • Product filter covers the top 100 brands by sales volume (~95% of units). Everything else is grouped as "Other" — no product is silently dropped, just folded in.
  • Procurement = all stock coming in — vendor purchases, own-plant transfers, and seed-channel adjustments — added together.
  • Clearance vs Fresh only covers barcoded (serialised) stock, and excludes plants and in-transit stock.
  • Clearance = less than 150 days left before expiry. Fresh = 150 days or more. Items with no expiry date (mostly hardware) are counted separately and folded into Fresh for the headline tiles.
