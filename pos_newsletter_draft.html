Magic Moment 01
The stock arrives.
The store is ready in minutes.
A truck pulls up. The store manager opens POS on their phone. They tap through the incoming inventory — batch by batch, good or damaged — and hit confirm. The warehouse system updates automatically. Every item is accounted for, shelved, and ready to sell. No calls. No email. No waiting.
  • 📦 Raise stock requests from the store — auto-converts to a warehouse transfer order
  • ✅ Receive incoming stock with good/bad capture per batch — warehouse inventory updates instantly
  • 🔄 Mark damaged inventory in POS — moves to the correct bin in the warehouse system automatically
Magic Moment 02
A farmer walks in.
Billed and out in under 3 minutes.
The manager searches the farmer by mobile number. Profile appears instantly — name, village, past orders. The farmer browses the catalog: live prices, active offers, real stock counts. They pick what they need. The system selects the right batch automatically. Invoice generated. Payment collected — cash or a QR code on screen. The ledger reconciles itself.
  • 🔍 Search any farmer by mobile — or register a new one instantly with a cascading pincode form
  • 🛒 Full catalog with live prices, offers, stock count — same data as the farmer app
  • 🧾 Invoice pushed to financial system automatically — zero manual entry
  • 💳 Cash (deposited to store VAN) or dynamic UPI QR — both reconcile to the ledger automatically
  • ↩️ Returns handled on the same screen — condition captured, credit posted automatically
Magic Moment 03
An online order just came in.
The store handles it.
A farmer in the store's catchment placed an order on the AgroStar app. It routes directly to the nearest COCO store. The manager sees it in POS, marks it available and triggers the invoice in one tap. The delivery partner picks it up. If it returns, the manager processes it on the same screen — return logged, inventory updated, credit posted.
  • 📲 DVS orders routed to the store appear directly in POS — accept, flag OOS, or process in one flow
  • 🚚 Invoice sent to warehouse and logistics automatically at dispatch
  • 🔁 Out-of-stock items trigger auto-restock — same engine as the Saathi partner network
Under the Hood
POS is not a billing app. It is the connective layer between three systems that had never spoken to each other in a retail context — our order management system, our warehouse platform, and our financial system. When a sale happens on POS, all three know about it instantly and automatically.
